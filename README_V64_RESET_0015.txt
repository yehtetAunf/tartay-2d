Tartay 2D v6.4 - 12:15 AM daily reset

Myanmar-time result-day rule:
- 12:00 AM is Round 8 / final round of the previous result day.
- 12:00:00 AM through 12:14:59 AM: previous result day remains on Today screen.
- From 12:15:00 AM: Today screen switches to the new calendar/result day.
- Therefore old-day SET/VALUE/2D results disappear from Today and remain in History.
- New day's 8 rounds begin as -- until their respective live/result states.
